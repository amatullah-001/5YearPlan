# 2026 Positioning

2026 is preparation, not Year 1.

## Priorities

- Confirm the working destination and life constraints.
- Map current knowledge, skills, projects, commitments, and resources.
- Keep only projects that serve a real dependency or provide meaningful practice.
- Identify foundational gaps.
- Establish a small, usable evidence/research workflow.
- Continue Nursing education.
- Use existing projects as evidence/practice where they genuinely fit.
- Avoid building a large productivity system before the architecture is settled.

## Explicitly avoid

- choosing a hyper-specific niche without evidence
- collecting courses as a substitute for competence
- turning every interest into a project
- predicting exact 2029–2031 tools and products
- designing a rigid daily schedule for years that have not happened yet
