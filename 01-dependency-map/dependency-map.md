# Dependency Map

## Top-level dependency

**2031 sustainable remote health professional practice/capability**
→ defined scope + credible qualification where required + demonstrated competence + real evidence + workable remote structure + sustainable capacity.

The exact form of practice remains adaptive until evidence, scope, qualification requirements, and life fit justify narrowing.

## Capability dependencies

### Health expertise
Depends on Nursing education, health sciences, clinical/public-health foundations, clinical reasoning, and continued learning.

### Nutrition
Depends on foundational nutrition knowledge, physiology/metabolism, evidence appraisal, investigation of professional scope, and formal nutrition/dietetics education if the eventual practice scope requires it.

### Natural/traditional health knowledge
Depends on structured study, understanding of traditional frameworks, evidence appraisal, safety/interaction knowledge, and scope/qualification investigation before professional application.

### Tibb Nabawi
Depends on sound Islamic learning, careful distinction between Prophetic attribution and later scholarly/medical interpretation, and separate evaluation of any health application.

Tibb Nabawi may remain personal Islamic learning and does not have to become professional scope.

### Evidence and research
Depends on research methods, information retrieval, critical appraisal, statistics/epidemiology, citation, and repeated appraisal of real papers.

### Whole-person reasoning and scope judgment
Depends on health knowledge, contextual assessment, evidence judgment, recognition of uncertainty, safety awareness, referral judgment, and understanding professional boundaries.

### Translation and communication
Depends on audience understanding, writing, explanation, visual communication, instructional/product design, and iterative testing.

### Digital delivery
Depends on product thinking, basic web/app literacy, interaction design, content structure, and collaboration with developers when deeper engineering is required.

### Remote collaboration
Depends on written communication, project coordination, professional reliability, asynchronous work habits, and clear handoffs.

## Convergence

The intended convergence is:

**health knowledge + evidence + whole-person reasoning + relevant nutrition/natural/traditional knowledge + translation + appropriate delivery → useful, safe, appropriately scoped health work.**

Education may be one form of delivery, but it is not assumed to be the centre of the professional identity.

The long-term value is not a collection of disconnected skills.
