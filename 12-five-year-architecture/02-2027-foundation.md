# 2027: Foundation & Capability Formation

## Why 2027 exists

2028 will require applied competence and real-world evidence. That cannot be built reliably on shallow knowledge, scattered interests, or passive course completion.

2027 therefore builds the foundations that will compound for the rest of the five years.

## Primary outcomes

By the end of 2027, the user should have:

- a substantially stronger Nursing/health knowledge base
- a repeatable evidence/research workflow
- early but deliberate nutrition knowledge
- structured exposure to Tibb Nabawi and broader natural/traditional health knowledge
- repeated practice translating complex health information
- initial digital delivery capability
- basic professional communication and remote-work habits
- early evidence of competence from real outputs
- clearer evidence about which problems and forms of work deserve further investigation

## Workstreams

### 1. Health expertise

Continue serious Nursing education and deliberately strengthen the underlying anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology, medical-surgical, maternal/child, public/community health, and epidemiological understanding required by the actual programme.

The goal is not simply passing courses. It is building a clinical and health foundation that can later support accurate assessment, communication, intervention, and referral.

### 2. Evidence and research

Learn to:

- formulate a focused health question
- search efficiently
- distinguish stronger from weaker evidence
- read research papers
- understand basic study designs and statistics
- extract what a source actually supports
- synthesize multiple sources
- cite accurately
- identify uncertainty and limits

This is a central bridge across Nursing, nutrition, and natural/traditional health knowledge.

### 3. Nutrition

Build foundations in nutrition, metabolism, food patterns, nutrient functions, and health relationships.

Do not choose a narrow nutrition identity yet. The purpose is to establish enough knowledge to investigate the field intelligently.

### 4. Tibb Nabawi and natural/traditional health

Study Tibb Nabawi as genuine Islamic learning, while separately learning how to evaluate health claims and applications.

Begin structured exposure to broader natural/traditional/complementary health approaches.

The aim is not to prove or dismiss a whole tradition. The aim is to learn how to distinguish source, interpretation, evidence, safety, scope, and uncertainty.

### 5. Translation and communication

Repeatedly practise the core skill:

> translating complex information into clear useful engaging and understandable actionable experience

Practise with real health topics and real non-specialist audiences.

### 6. Digital delivery

Learn enough about digital health experiences to understand:

- information architecture
- user needs
- interaction
- content structure
- basic implementation
- testing
- collaboration with technical specialists

Digital remains a means, not a second career.

### 7. Professional exposure

Begin small.

Practise:

- professional written communication
- asynchronous collaboration
- receiving and incorporating feedback
- documenting work clearly
- understanding what remote health-related work actually asks of people

This is exposure, not a demand to obtain a job in 2027.

### 8. Projects

Use a small number of existing or newly justified projects as practice grounds.

A project earns its place when it develops a required capability, produces evidence, serves a real user, or reveals useful information about the direction.

## Evidence expected by year-end

Examples:

- strong academic work
- evidence summaries
- literature/appraisal exercises
- clear patient-facing explanations
- useful health resources or interventions
- a small digital prototype or meaningful contribution
- documented feedback and iteration
- evidence of reliable remote collaboration on at least a small scale

The quantity is less important than whether the evidence demonstrates increasing capability.

## Gate into 2028

2027 is successful if the user can show meaningful growth in health knowledge, evidence use, translation, nutrition foundations, and responsible engagement with natural/traditional health knowledge, while beginning to demonstrate real-world and remote-working capability.

If a major weakness remains, 2028 should repair it rather than blindly following the calendar.
