# Dependency Matrix

This matrix is the compact view of what each period must hand to the next.

| Period | Builds | Needed by | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct–Dec 2026 | Baseline, cleaned commitments, research workflow, clarified direction | 2027 | baseline + 2027 starting brief |
| 2027 | Health foundation, evidence literacy, nutrition foundation, translation practice, digital literacy | 2028 | academic/application work + early portfolio |
| 2028 | Applied competence, qualification progress, portfolio, audience/problem evidence | 2029 | real outputs + feedback + documented process |
| 2029 | Specialization, professional scope clarity, stronger collaboration, qualification decisions | 2030 | substantial work + external evidence |
| 2030 | Professional delivery, remote collaboration, sustainability testing | 2031 | credible body of work + workable model |
| 2031 | Consolidated capability and sustainable structure | continuation beyond 2031 | professional scope + evidence + life-compatible model |

## Cross-year dependencies

### Health knowledge
Starts before 2027 and compounds every year. It cannot be postponed until a specialization is chosen.

### Evidence literacy
Begins in 2026, becomes deliberate in 2027, embedded in work in 2028, and supports specialization from 2029 onward.

### Nutrition
Begins as foundational learning and investigation. It becomes deeper only if evidence and scope justify it.

### Translation
Begins immediately and requires repeated practice across all five years.

### Digital delivery
Begins as literacy and practice, becomes application in 2028, and becomes professional delivery only if the chosen direction requires it.

### Portfolio
Begins with small evidence in 2027, becomes intentional in 2028, becomes coherent in 2029, and becomes professional evidence in 2030–2031.

### Remote-life compatibility
Is tested continuously, not postponed until marriage or 2031.

## Critical rule

A later-year activity that depends on a capability not yet developed should either:
- move earlier as foundational practice,
- be supported by an appropriate collaborator,
- or be deferred.

Do not solve a dependency problem by pretending the dependency does not exist.
