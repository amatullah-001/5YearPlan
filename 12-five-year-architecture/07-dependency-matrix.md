# Dependency Matrix

This matrix is the compact view of what each period must hand to the next.

| Period | Builds | Needed by | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct–Dec 2026 | Baseline, evidence workflow, scope/qualification map, separated knowledge domains, manageable workload | 2027 | baseline + 2027 starting brief |
| 2027 | Nursing/health foundation, evidence discipline, nutrition/Tibb/traditional foundations, translation, digital literacy, remote-work habits | 2028 | academic/application work + early real outputs |
| 2028 | Applied competence, qualification progress, portfolio, external feedback, small collaboration, scope evidence, maintenance testing | 2029 | real outputs + feedback + documented process |
| 2029 | Deeper specialization, professional scope, referral boundaries, qualification decisions, stronger collaboration | 2030 | substantial work + external evidence |
| 2030 | Professional delivery, remote collaboration, reliability, reach, sustainability and maintenance testing | 2031 | credible body of work + workable model |
| 2031 | Consolidated capability and sustainable structure | continuation beyond 2031 | professional scope + evidence + life-compatible model |

## Cross-year dependencies

### Health and Nursing
Starts before 2027 and compounds every year. It cannot be postponed until a specialization is chosen.

### Evidence and research
Begins in 2026, becomes deliberate in 2027, embedded in work in 2028, and supports specialization and safety judgment from 2029 onward.

### Nutrition
Begins as foundational learning and investigation. It becomes deeper only if evidence, scope, usefulness, and qualification requirements justify it.

### Tibb Nabawi
Begins as genuine Islamic learning and remains valuable even if it never becomes professional practice.

### Natural/traditional health
Begins as structured investigation. It becomes professional only if the eventual scope, training, evidence, safety, regulation, and life fit justify that move.

### Whole-person reasoning
Begins with Nursing/health foundations and becomes more sophisticated through actual cases/problems, evidence appraisal, and scope judgment.

### Translation
Begins immediately and requires repeated practice across all five years.

### Digital delivery
Begins as literacy and small practice, becomes application in 2028, and becomes professional delivery only if the chosen direction requires it.

### Remote professional capability
Begins as communication and asynchronous collaboration practice in 2027, small collaborations in 2028, deliberate professional collaboration in 2029, and substantial delivery in 2030.

### Portfolio
Begins with small evidence in 2027, becomes intentional in 2028, becomes coherent in 2029, and becomes professional evidence in 2030–2031.

### Life compatibility
Is tested continuously. Workload, maintenance, availability, and family-season resilience are design constraints from the beginning, not final checks.

## Critical rule

A later-year activity that depends on a capability not yet developed should either:

- move earlier as foundational practice,
- be supported by an appropriate collaborator,
- or be deferred.

Do not solve a dependency problem by pretending the dependency does not exist.
