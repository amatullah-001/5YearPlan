# 2029: Evidence-Led Specialization

## Why 2029 exists

By 2029, the user should have enough accumulated knowledge, practice, feedback, and scope information to stop treating every possibility as equally open.

Specialization now becomes an evidence-based narrowing process.

## Primary outcomes

By the end of 2029, the user should have:

- a clearly defined professional problem space
- a defensible deeper domain or interdisciplinary configuration
- strong supporting domains
- clear scope and referral boundaries
- identified qualification gaps
- repeated evidence of usefulness
- stronger external collaboration evidence
- a remote work model that has been tested rather than imagined

## Specialization process

Specialization should be based on the intersection of:

**What the user can become very good at**
+
**What people actually need**
+
**What evidence supports**
+
**What fits the intended remote life structure**
+
**What the user's professional/ethical scope permits**
+
**What training is legitimately required**

Interest alone is not enough. Neither is market appeal alone.

## Possible depth configurations

The architecture may now support a decision such as:

- Nursing as the deep professional domain, with nutrition and natural/traditional health as strong supporting domains.
- Nutrition as the deep professional domain, with Nursing and natural/traditional health supporting it.
- A formally trained natural/integrative pathway, if legitimate training, regulation, evidence, safety, and life fit justify it.
- Research/evidence as a major professional capability around a health domain.
- A genuinely interdisciplinary role that does not fit an existing label neatly.

The architecture does not force one configuration in advance.

## Work

- Deepen the health problem space that repeatedly proves useful.
- Deepen nutrition only to the level justified by evidence and professional scope.
- Deepen natural/traditional health knowledge only where it has a legitimate role in the emerging work.
- Strengthen research/evidence skills around the chosen problem space.
- Build more sophisticated interventions, programmes, digital experiences, or appropriately scoped individualized work.
- Seek appropriate remote collaborations where they expose the user to real problems and standards.
- Investigate postgraduate or specialist qualifications where a genuine capability or scope gap justifies them.
- Reduce peripheral projects that no longer serve the emerging direction.

## Evidence expected

Examples:

- substantial real work
- evidence syntheses
- intervention/product work
- documented user feedback
- collaboration deliverables
- qualifications where required
- references or other credible evidence of reliability and competence
- clear documentation of what is within and outside scope

## Gate into 2030

The user should be able to state, without pretending certainty where it does not exist:

- the professional problem space she is building around
- the deeper domain or configuration she is pursuing
- the capabilities that distinguish her work
- the boundaries of her scope
- the qualification gaps that matter
- the kind of remote collaboration or work she is ready to pursue
- what she will deliberately stop doing

2029 is the year of informed narrowing, not permanent self-definition.
