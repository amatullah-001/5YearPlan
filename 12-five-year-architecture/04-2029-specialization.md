# 2029: Evidence-Led Specialization

## Why 2029 exists

By 2029, the user should have enough accumulated knowledge, practice, and feedback to stop treating every possibility as equally open.

Specialization now becomes an evidence-based narrowing process.

## Primary outcomes

By the end of 2029, the user should have:

- a clearer professional intersection of health, nutrition, evidence, translation, and digital delivery
- a body of work showing repeated usefulness in that intersection
- stronger external feedback or collaboration evidence
- identified qualification gaps
- a reasoned decision about what to deepen and what to leave at supporting-skill level

## Specialization process

Specialization should be based on the intersection of:

**What the user can become very good at**
+
**What people actually need**
+
**What evidence supports**
+
**What fits the intended remote life structure**
+
**What the user's professional/ethical scope permits**

Interest alone is not enough. Neither is market appeal alone.

## Work

- Deepen the health domain that repeatedly proves useful.
- Deepen nutrition only to the level justified by evidence and professional scope.
- Strengthen research/evidence skills around the chosen problem space.
- Build more sophisticated educational or digital interventions.
- Seek appropriate remote collaborations where they expose the user to real problems and standards.
- Investigate postgraduate or specialist qualifications only where a genuine capability or scope gap justifies them.
- Reduce peripheral projects that no longer serve the emerging direction.

## Evidence expected

Examples:
- substantial educational work
- evidence syntheses
- intervention/product work
- documented user feedback
- collaboration deliverables
- academic/professional qualifications where required
- references or other credible evidence of reliability and competence

## Gate into 2030

The user should be able to state, without pretending certainty where it does not exist:

- the professional problem space she is building around
- the capabilities that distinguish her work
- the boundaries of her scope
- the qualification gaps that matter
- the kind of remote collaboration or work she is ready to pursue
- what she will deliberately stop doing

2029 is the year of informed narrowing, not permanent self-definition.
