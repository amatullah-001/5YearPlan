# Prerequisite Branches

Some future decisions cannot be fixed in 2026, but their prerequisites can be investigated early.

## Branch A: Nursing-led health practice/intervention

If Nursing remains the deep professional domain:

- Nursing qualification remains the professional foundation.
- Evidence/research and whole-person reasoning become major supporting competencies.
- Nutrition can remain a supporting domain unless deeper credentials become necessary.
- Natural/traditional health knowledge can remain supporting knowledge unless a legitimate, evidence- and scope-supported role emerges.
- Education and digital delivery remain possible methods of delivering useful work rather than defining the identity.

## Branch B: Nutrition becomes a major professional domain

If evidence later shows that nutrition should become a major part of the professional scope:

- determine the relevant professional boundaries
- identify the appropriate formal education/credential requirements
- compare the required depth with the user's intended work
- determine whether further training is actually necessary
- only then commit substantial time to the pathway

## Branch C: Natural/integrative health becomes a deeper domain

If natural or integrative health becomes a serious professional direction:

- identify the exact scope being contemplated rather than treating “natural health” as one profession
- investigate legitimate education and credential pathways
- establish applicable professional/legal boundaries
- assess evidence and safety standards for the practices involved
- determine supervised/practical requirements where relevant
- test compatibility with the intended remote/family structure
- commit only if the resulting scope is legitimate, useful, evidence-responsible, and life-compatible

## Branch D: Research/evidence becomes central

If the work increasingly depends on research:

- deepen research methods
- strengthen statistics/epidemiology
- develop literature synthesis and critical appraisal
- investigate appropriate academic/professional pathways
- build supervised or collaborative research evidence where relevant

## Branch E: Digital health becomes central

If digital health delivery becomes the dominant mode:

- deepen product/user research
- interaction and information architecture
- prototyping
- evaluation
- technical collaboration
- only develop deeper software engineering if the work demonstrates that it is needed

## Branch F: Independent remote work becomes central

If independent work becomes the practical model:

- develop professional communication
- scope work clearly
- document deliverables
- learn basic project/contract/client practices as appropriate
- test maintenance and income reliability before depending on the model

## Branch rule

Do not choose a branch because it sounds attractive.

Choose it when accumulated evidence shows that the branch is useful, feasible, within scope, legitimately trainable where necessary, and compatible with the life being built.
