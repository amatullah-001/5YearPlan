# Prerequisite Branches

Some future decisions cannot be fixed in 2026, but their prerequisites can be investigated early.

## Branch A: Nursing-led health education

If the eventual scope remains primarily health education grounded in Nursing:
- Nursing qualification remains the professional foundation.
- Evidence/research and communication become major supporting competencies.
- Nutrition can remain a supporting domain unless deeper credentials become necessary.
- Digital delivery can remain a delivery capability.

## Branch B: Nutrition becomes a major professional domain

If evidence later shows that nutrition should become a major part of the professional scope:
- determine the relevant professional boundaries
- identify the appropriate formal education/credential requirements
- compare the required depth with the user's intended work
- determine whether further training is actually necessary
- only then commit substantial time to the pathway

## Branch C: Research/evidence becomes central

If the work increasingly depends on research:
- deepen research methods
- strengthen statistics/epidemiology
- develop literature synthesis and critical appraisal
- investigate appropriate academic/professional pathways
- build supervised or collaborative research evidence where relevant

## Branch D: Digital health becomes central

If digital health delivery becomes the dominant mode:
- deepen product/user research
- interaction and information architecture
- prototyping
- evaluation
- technical collaboration
- only develop deeper software engineering if the work demonstrates that it is needed

## Branch E: Independent remote work becomes central

If independent work becomes the practical model:
- develop professional communication
- scope work clearly
- document deliverables
- learn basic project/contract/client practices as appropriate
- test maintenance and income reliability before depending on the model

## Branch rule

Do not choose the branch because it sounds attractive.

Choose it when accumulated evidence shows that the branch is useful, feasible, within scope, and compatible with the life being built.
