# Architecture Stress Test

This document tests whether the architecture survives the question:

**“What must be true next year, and what must therefore be built this year?”**

## Test 1: 2031 must not depend on 2030 suddenly creating competence

### 2031 requires
- credible professional scope
- demonstrated competence
- real work evidence
- remote work capability
- sustainable work structure
- appropriate qualifications

### Therefore 2030 must already provide
- substantial delivery
- professional reliability
- remote collaboration
- sustainability testing
- qualification completion/progress where needed

### Therefore 2029 must already provide
- enough specialization clarity to know what work to pursue
- evidence that the user's capability is useful
- external feedback/collaboration
- identified scope and credential gaps

### Therefore 2028 must already provide
- repeated application
- portfolio-quality outputs
- actual audience/problem exposure
- stronger evidence use
- enough experience to distinguish promising directions from merely interesting ones

### Therefore 2027 must provide
- strong foundations
- deliberate practice
- research/evidence literacy
- nutrition foundations
- translation practice
- initial digital delivery practice
- early evidence of competence

### Therefore Oct–Dec 2026 must provide
- baseline
- prerequisite map
- resource/commitment cleanup
- a usable evidence workflow
- initial practice
- clarity about what will actually be carried into 2027

**Result: PASS, with earlier remote/professional exposure strengthened below.**

---

## Test 2: Can 2028 actually produce the portfolio promised?

A portfolio cannot be created merely by deciding to make one in 2028.

It requires:
- capability
- real problems/audiences
- evidence
- repeated iteration
- enough technical/digital ability to produce the chosen form
- documentation

Therefore:
- 2027 must begin producing small pieces of evidence, not merely studying.
- 2026 should identify existing projects that can become legitimate practice grounds.
- 2028 should select and deepen evidence rather than start portfolio-building from zero.

**Result: PASS.**

---

## Test 3: Can 2029 specialization be evidence-led?

Specialization requires evidence about:
- capability
- usefulness
- interest
- audience/problem fit
- professional scope
- qualification requirements
- remote-life compatibility

If these are first investigated in 2029, specialization becomes guesswork.

Therefore:
- 2027 begins broad exposure.
- 2028 deliberately gathers real-world evidence.
- 2029 interprets that evidence and narrows.

**Result: PASS.**

---

## Test 4: Can nutrition remain honest?

The architecture currently risks making “nutrition” look like a predetermined professional specialty.

That is too strong.

The correct dependency is:

**nutrition foundation → investigation → applied use within current scope → evidence of fit → decision about deeper study/credential → deeper specialization only if justified.**

Therefore nutrition must remain a domain being investigated until the evidence supports a stronger commitment.

**Result: REFINEMENT REQUIRED.**

---

## Test 5: Can digital work remain a tool rather than become a second career?

There is a risk that “digital delivery” expands into unnecessary software engineering.

The actual need is:

**understand users + structure information + design useful experiences + prototype/test + collaborate with technical specialists when necessary.**

Deeper engineering should only be developed when the chosen professional direction genuinely requires it.

**Result: PASS, but keep scope narrow.**

---

## Test 6: Can remote work really be assumed?

No.

A remote-only professional structure needs evidence that the user can:
- work asynchronously
- communicate professionally
- manage deliverables
- collaborate remotely
- produce useful work without physical supervision
- understand what remote roles actually require

Therefore remote capability must begin being practised before 2030.

**Result: REFINEMENT REQUIRED.**

---

## Test 7: Can sustainability really wait until 2030?

No.

If the intended professional structure must fit worship, home, family, health, rest, and future motherhood, waiting until 2030 to discover that a work model consumes too much capacity is too late.

Therefore:
- workload/capacity awareness begins in 2026
- sustainable study/work patterns are observed in 2027
- project maintenance and remote-work burden are tested in 2028
- professional sustainability is tested more seriously in 2029
- 2030 validates the model at substantial scale

**Result: REFINEMENT REQUIRED.**

---

## Test 8: Does qualification planning start too late?

It could.

The exact future credential cannot be chosen yet, but the user should investigate prerequisites early enough to avoid discovering in 2029 that a desired scope requires years of training.

Therefore:
- 2026 identifies possible qualification branches and unknowns.
- 2027 investigates relevant professional boundaries and prerequisites.
- 2028 narrows the credential question.
- 2029 makes the decision when the scope is sufficiently evidenced.
- 2030 executes the necessary qualification path where appropriate.

**Result: REFINEMENT REQUIRED.**

---

## Test 9: Does professional network/collaboration appear too late?

Yes, if interpreted literally.

Professional collaboration should not suddenly begin in 2030.

The architecture should distinguish:
- early exposure to people and standards
- small collaborations
- substantial professional collaboration

Therefore:
- 2027: observe and practise professional communication.
- 2028: appropriate small collaborations/feedback.
- 2029: deliberate remote collaboration.
- 2030: substantial professional collaboration.

**Result: REFINEMENT REQUIRED.**

---

## Test 10: Are we overloading 2027?

Potentially.

2027 currently contains Nursing knowledge, research, nutrition, communication, digital delivery, projects, and portfolio evidence.

The answer is not to remove foundational capabilities. It is to distinguish **depth from exposure**.

In 2027:
- Nursing/health knowledge is the major academic foundation.
- Evidence/research and translation receive deliberate repeated practice.
- Nutrition is foundational investigation.
- Digital delivery is literacy and small practice.
- Portfolio is evidence generated from the work, not a separate workload.
- Projects remain few.

**Result: PASS after clarification.**

---

## Test 11: Are we over-specifying future years?

No major problem remains.

Exact employers, clients, software, products, postgraduate courses, niche, and income model remain adaptive.

**Result: PASS.**

---

## Stress-test conclusion

The architecture is structurally sound, but four changes are required:

1. Start qualification/scope investigation earlier.
2. Start remote collaboration and professional exposure earlier.
3. Test life-compatible workload and maintenance earlier.
4. Keep nutrition explicitly conditional rather than treating it as a guaranteed specialization.

The important finding is that the architecture should not become more detailed by inventing hundreds of future tasks. It should become more detailed by making the **dependencies and gates more precise**.
