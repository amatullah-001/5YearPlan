# October–December 2026: Groundwork

These three months are preparation for 2027. They are not a miniature version of 2027.

The purpose is to make January 2027 a clean starting point rather than beginning the five-year build while the foundations are still unclear.

## October: establish the ground

### Main purpose
Know what is actually being built and what is currently available.

### Work
- Read and understand the complete 2026–2031 architecture.
- Map the current Nursing/health knowledge baseline.
- Identify the strongest current capabilities and the weakest prerequisites.
- Inventory existing projects and decide what genuinely belongs in the five-year direction.
- Organize useful learning resources without creating a large new course list.
- Establish a simple research/evidence workflow: finding sources, recording them, understanding what they support, and citing them accurately.
- Keep current academic responsibilities central.

### Output
A clear baseline: current capability, current evidence, gaps, active projects, useful resources, and unnecessary commitments.

## November: test the foundations

### Main purpose
Begin practising the core capabilities at low scale.

### Work
- Practise translating health information into clear patient-facing explanations.
- Complete a small number of evidence-appraisal exercises using real health sources.
- Study nutrition from sound foundational sources without choosing a specialty yet.
- Use one existing project as a practice ground for health education, content structure, or digital delivery.
- Notice which kinds of health problems, audiences, and work repeatedly hold genuine interest and usefulness.
- Identify any academic or professional prerequisite that needs early attention.

### Output
Early evidence of how the user learns, researches, explains, builds, and works.

## December: close the loop

### Main purpose
Turn the groundwork into a clean 2027 starting position.

### Work
- Review October and November evidence rather than judging progress by activity alone.
- Record what improved and what remained weak.
- Remove commitments that create fragmentation without serving a dependency.
- Decide the small set of foundational capabilities that will receive sustained attention in 2027.
- Define the first 2027 learning/application sequence without pretending to know every task for the year.
- Confirm that the plan still fits worship, family, health, rest, household life, and academic demands.

### Output
A 2027 starting brief containing:
- baseline
- priority capabilities
- known prerequisites
- initial evidence
- active project(s)
- resources
- open questions to investigate
- things deliberately left undecided

## What 2026 is not for

- proving the final career niche
- becoming an expert in nutrition
- building a large portfolio
- collecting many certificates
- launching multiple products
- designing the entire 2031 daily schedule
- turning life into a productivity dashboard

The win is entering 2027 with the ground clear and the direction understood.
