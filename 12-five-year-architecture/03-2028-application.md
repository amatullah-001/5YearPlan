# 2028: Application, Qualification Progress & Portfolio

## Why 2028 exists

2029 will require enough real evidence to decide where deeper specialization is justified.

2028 therefore moves from mostly foundational development toward repeated application.

## Primary outcomes

By the end of 2028, the user should have:

- continued progress toward the Nursing qualification on the actual programme timeline
- stronger independent evidence use
- repeated real-world health education/application
- a growing body of portfolio evidence
- clearer understanding of users/problems she can serve well
- stronger digital delivery ability
- a more informed view of nutrition as a possible deeper domain

## Workstreams

### Health and qualification
Maintain academic progression while increasingly connecting formal Nursing knowledge to health education and public-facing work.

The qualification remains a real professional foundation, not merely a line on a CV.

### Evidence
Move from practising appraisal to using evidence as part of actual work.

For substantial outputs, the user should increasingly be able to explain:
- what evidence was used
- why it was relevant
- what it does and does not establish
- how it changed the final educational or intervention design

### Translation
Produce work for actual audiences rather than only exercises.

Iterate from feedback. Test whether people understand the intended message and can act on it appropriately.

### Digital delivery
Build or contribute to increasingly coherent digital experiences.

The target is not “become a software developer.” The target is being capable of shaping useful digital health education and collaborating effectively on implementation.

### Nutrition
Continue structured investigation. Compare the user's interest, competence, evidence base, and possible professional requirements before committing to a formal specialization.

### Portfolio
Begin treating selected work as professional evidence.

Document:
- problem
- audience
- evidence
- design/approach
- implementation
- feedback
- changes
- result or learning

## Gate into 2029

By the end of 2028, there should be enough evidence to answer more intelligently:

- Which health problems do I repeatedly understand and explain well?
- Which audiences benefit from my work?
- Where does nutrition genuinely strengthen the work?
- What kind of digital delivery fits my capability?
- What professional qualification or credential is actually needed for the direction that is emerging?

The answer does not have to be a final niche. It must be better evidenced than it was in 2026.
