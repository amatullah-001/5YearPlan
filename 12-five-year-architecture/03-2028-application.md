# 2028: Application, Qualification Progress & Portfolio

## Why 2028 exists

2029 will require enough real evidence to decide where deeper specialization is justified.

2028 therefore moves from mostly foundational development toward repeated application and external feedback.

## Primary outcomes

By the end of 2028, the user should have:

- continued progress toward the Nursing qualification on the actual programme timeline
- stronger independent evidence use
- repeated application to real health problems
- a growing body of portfolio evidence
- clearer understanding of users/problems she can serve well
- stronger digital delivery ability
- small remote collaboration experience
- a more informed view of nutrition and natural/traditional health as possible deeper domains
- better understanding of qualification and scope requirements

## Workstreams

### Health and qualification

Maintain academic progression while increasingly connecting formal Nursing knowledge to actual health problems and useful interventions.

The qualification remains a real professional foundation, not merely a line on a CV.

### Evidence

Move from practising appraisal to using evidence as part of actual work.

For substantial outputs, the user should increasingly be able to explain:

- what evidence was used
- why it was relevant
- what it does and does not establish
- how it changed the final work
- what remains uncertain
- whether the proposed action is within scope

### Translation and intervention

Produce work for actual audiences rather than only exercises.

Education may be one output, but it is not assumed to be the final centre of the professional direction.

Other possible outputs include structured interventions, programmes, digital experiences, evidence syntheses, or appropriately scoped individualized work.

Iterate from feedback.

### Natural/traditional health and Tibb Nabawi

Continue structured learning without collapsing the categories.

Tibb Nabawi remains valid Islamic learning in its own right.

Natural/traditional health approaches are investigated for usefulness, evidence, safety, and scope.

### Digital delivery

Build or contribute to increasingly coherent digital experiences.

The target is not “become a software developer.” The target is being capable of shaping useful health experiences and collaborating effectively on implementation.

### Qualification and scope investigation

By 2028, begin narrowing which formal credentials are actually required for the emerging scope.

Do not acquire credentials merely to make the CV look fuller.

### Portfolio

Treat selected work as professional evidence.

Document:

- problem
- context/audience
- evidence
- design/approach
- implementation
- feedback
- changes
- result or learning
- scope/referral considerations where relevant

## Life-compatibility test

Do not wait until 2030.

Observe:

- how much maintenance a project creates
- whether work can be put down
- whether remote collaboration becomes intrusive
- whether output quality collapses under ordinary academic/family demands
- which forms of work give useful reach without requiring continuous availability

This is design evidence.

## Gate into 2029

By the end of 2028, there should be enough evidence to answer more intelligently:

- Which health problems do I repeatedly understand and address well?
- Which audiences benefit from my work?
- Where does nutrition genuinely strengthen the work?
- Where do natural/traditional approaches add useful knowledge, if anywhere?
- What kind of digital delivery fits my capability?
- What professional qualification or credential is actually needed?
- What should remain personal/supporting knowledge rather than become professional scope?

The answer does not have to be a final niche. It must be better evidenced than it was in 2026.
