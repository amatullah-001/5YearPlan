# Required Capabilities

By 2031, the user should be able to:

1. Understand health problems deeply enough to contribute accurately and appropriately within her scope.
2. Consider the person's relevant context rather than treating every health problem as interchangeable.
3. Understand nutrition deeply enough to use it responsibly within her professional scope.
4. Understand natural/traditional health approaches well enough to distinguish useful, uncertain, unsupported, unsafe, and out-of-scope applications.
5. Find and evaluate evidence rather than relying on summaries.
6. Synthesize evidence into useful conclusions while preserving uncertainty and limitations.
7. Translate complex information into clear useful engaging and understandable actionable experience.
8. Design or contribute to useful health interventions, programmes, resources, or digital experiences; education is one possible form.
9. Recognize when individualized attention, intervention, or referral is appropriate within scope.
10. Communicate and collaborate effectively in remote teams.
11. Produce evidence of competence through real work, not only completed courses.
12. Maintain professional work without allowing it to consume the life it is meant to fit inside.
