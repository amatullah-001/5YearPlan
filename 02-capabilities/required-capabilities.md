# Required Capabilities

By 2031, the user should be able to:

1. Understand health problems deeply enough to educate accurately within her scope.
2. Understand nutrition deeply enough to use it responsibly within her professional scope.
3. Find and evaluate evidence rather than relying on summaries.
4. Synthesize evidence into useful conclusions.
5. Translate complex evidence into clear useful engaging and understandable actionable experience.
6. Design educational experiences for non-specialist audiences.
7. Design or contribute to digital health interventions/products.
8. Communicate and collaborate effectively in remote teams.
9. Evaluate whether an intervention or educational product actually helps.
10. Maintain professional work without allowing it to consume the life it is meant to fit inside.
