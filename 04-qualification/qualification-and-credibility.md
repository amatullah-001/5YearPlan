# Qualification & Credibility

Qualification requirements must be verified when the intended scope becomes clearer.

## Fixed principle

Do not assume that a Nursing qualification automatically authorizes every future nutrition, dietetics, research, health-coaching, or clinical intervention role.

## Track

- Nursing qualification and professional requirements
- Any nutrition/dietetics qualification required for intended scope
- Research/academic credentials only when they serve a real future need
- Portfolio evidence
- Relevant supervised experience where required
- Professional standards and legal/regulatory boundaries

The exact credential path stays adaptive until the intended scope is sufficiently evidenced.
