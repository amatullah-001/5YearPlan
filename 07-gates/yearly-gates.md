# Gates

A gate is a condition, not merely a deadline.

## 2026 → 2027

- Destination and constraints are clear enough.
- Current baseline and major gaps are known.
- Existing commitments/resources have been cleaned up.
- The architecture is understandable without requiring a daily productivity system.

## 2027 → 2028

- Stronger health foundation.
- Sustained practice of the core translation skill.
- Initial evidence/research literacy.
- Serious nutrition investigation underway.
- Early portfolio evidence.

## 2028 → 2029

- Nursing qualification progress consistent with the actual programme.
- Stronger independent evidence use.
- Multiple real outputs.
- Clearer understanding of which health problems/audiences are worth pursuing.
- Evidence about whether nutrition should become a deeper specialization.

## 2029 → 2030

- Clearer professional intersection.
- Demonstrated ability to produce useful work.
- Evidence from real collaboration/users.
- Identified gaps that justify further qualification or specialization.

## 2030 → 2031

- Credible professional scope.
- Portfolio/body of work.
- Remote collaboration or work evidence.
- Sustainable work model compatible with intended life structure.
- Clear maintenance burden.
- Enough evidence to deepen rather than simply accumulate options.
