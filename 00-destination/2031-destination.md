# 2031 Destination

The 2031 destination is a set of conditions that should be true, not a predetermined job title.

## Professional

By 2031, the user should have:

- Completed her Nursing qualification on the timeline and requirements of her actual programme.
- A clearly defined remote health-professional scope based on what she has learned, built, qualified for, and found meaningful.
- Strong health knowledge grounded in nursing and continued evidence-based learning.
- A defined level of nutrition expertise. If the eventual scope requires professional nutrition/dietetics practice, the appropriate formal qualification and legal/professional requirements must be identified and met rather than assumed.
- Independent ability to find, appraise, synthesize, cite, and apply health evidence.
- Strong ability to translate complex information into clear useful engaging and understandable actionable experience.
- Ability to design or contribute to digital health education/intervention experiences.
- A body of real work demonstrating competence.
- Experience collaborating remotely with relevant public-health, health-technology, education, research, or NGO teams where appropriate.
- A sustainable way to earn or contribute professionally without requiring a physical workplace.

## Life

The professional structure should be compatible with the life the user wants: marriage, motherhood, home, worship, family, health, rest, relationships, and ordinary living.

Professional success is not the sole measure of whether the five years went well.

## Intentionally undecided

The exact specialty, employer/client model, postgraduate qualification, products, business model, software stack, and future projects should not be fixed in 2026 unless evidence makes them necessary.
