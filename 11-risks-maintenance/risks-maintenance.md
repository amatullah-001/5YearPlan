# Risks & Maintenance

## Fragmentation
Too many projects or interests can consume the attention required to build the core capability.

**Countermeasure:** every project must justify its place by a dependency, practice value, evidence value, or genuine service value.

## Course accumulation
Learning resources can create the feeling of progress without corresponding capability.

**Countermeasure:** pair substantial learning with application and evidence.

## Premature specialization
A niche chosen before enough exposure can become a constraint.

**Countermeasure:** specialize from evidence gathered through 2027–2029.

## Maintenance tax
Digital products, public content, communities, and other assets can require ongoing work.

**Countermeasure:** assess maintenance burden before committing; do not build assets that compete with the capability they were supposed to support.

## Life incompatibility
A professional structure can become successful on paper while being incompatible with the intended family and home life.

**Countermeasure:** treat remote work and future household compatibility as design requirements from the beginning.

## Skill acquisition latency
Important skills take repeated practice.

**Countermeasure:** start foundational capabilities early and allow years for depth rather than compressing them into short courses.

## Final test

If a new commitment does not clearly support the destination, a required competency, evidence of competence, a necessary qualification, or meaningful service, it should face a high bar for inclusion.
