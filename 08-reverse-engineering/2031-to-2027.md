# Reverse Engineering: 2031 → 2027

## 2031

Need a sustainable remote health professional structure.

Therefore: defined scope, credible standing where required, demonstrated competence, real work evidence, sustainable income/contribution model, and compatibility with housewife/mother life.

## 2030

To establish that structure: build substantial work, collaborate remotely, test sustainability, strengthen the professional intersection, and resolve qualification gaps revealed by actual scope.

## 2029

To build substantial work: specialize enough to solve meaningful problems, integrate health + evidence + nutrition + translation + digital capabilities, gather external feedback, and identify gaps that require deeper study.

## 2028

To specialize responsibly: deepen health/evidence foundations, apply skills in real projects, understand professional/legal scope, accumulate portfolio evidence, and observe where repeated usefulness is emerging.

## 2027

To make 2028 possible: establish the core knowledge base, deliberately train the translation skill, begin systematic research/evidence practice, investigate nutrition rigorously, and use projects as practice grounds rather than collecting projects for their own sake.

## 2026

To make 2027 possible: clarify destination, establish baseline, clean existing commitments, organize resources, identify prerequisites, and avoid premature specialization.
