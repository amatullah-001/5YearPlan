# Feedback & Adaptation

The architecture is fixed enough to guide direction, but adaptive enough to respond to evidence.

## Review questions

1. What can I now do that I could not do before?
2. What evidence proves it?
3. Which assumptions about the destination were confirmed?
4. Which assumptions were weakened or disproved?
5. What real problems/audiences have I repeatedly found useful to?
6. What feedback changed the work?
7. What qualification or credential is now actually necessary?
8. What maintenance burden has accumulated?
9. Does the professional structure still fit the life I am trying to build?
10. What should stop, continue, deepen, or remain undecided?

## Fixed vs adaptive

**Fixed:** destination, remote-only structure, intended housewife/mother compatibility, core direction, major competencies, necessary prerequisites.

**Adaptive:** exact courses, projects, tools, clients/collaborators, products, monthly goals, daily tasks, specialization details.
