# Evidence of Competence

Courses are inputs. Evidence is what shows that capability has actually developed.

## Evidence types

- accurate explanations
- evidence syntheses
- research/appraisal work
- educational content
- intervention designs
- prototypes/products
- user feedback
- measurable comprehension or behaviour outcomes when appropriate
- collaborative deliverables
- qualifications
- professional references

## Rule

Do not count “I completed a course” as equivalent to “I can perform the capability.”

Each year should produce evidence that changes what the user can credibly say she can do.
