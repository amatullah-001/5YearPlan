# Five-Year Life & Professional Architecture

This repository is the working source of truth for the user's 2026 preparation and 2027–2031 direction.

## Purpose

Build a professional capability that can live inside the life the user actually wants: a Muslim woman preparing for marriage and motherhood, with a remote-only professional structure.

Working direction:

> Developing the knowledge, clinical grounding, evidence judgment and practical capability to understand and address health problems through Nursing, nutrition and natural/traditional health knowledge, and to translate that capability into useful care, interventions and experiences that can reach people remotely and remain compatible with the life Allah has given me.

This is a working direction, not a premature commitment to a job title or specialty.

Core skill:

> translating complex information into clear useful engaging and understandable actionable experience

## Time horizon

- **October–December 2026:** preparation and positioning. These three months are active groundwork, but are not counted as Year 1.
- **2027–2031:** the five-year build.
- The architecture is reverse-engineered from what must be true in 2031.

## Actual architecture

1. **October–December 2026:** establish the ground, test foundations, close the loop.
2. **2027:** foundation and deliberate capability formation.
3. **2028:** application, qualification progress, and portfolio formation.
4. **2029:** evidence-led specialization and professional direction.
5. **2030:** substantial remote work, collaboration, and professional establishment.
6. **2031:** sustainable professional structure and consolidation.

Each period exists because the next period depends on what it builds.

## Core rule

Every component must answer:

**What future capability or life condition does this enable?**

If it cannot answer that, it probably does not belong in the architecture.

## Fixed vs adaptive

**Fixed:** destination, remote-only structure, intended housewife/mother compatibility, core direction, major competencies, and necessary prerequisites.

**Adaptive:** exact courses, projects, tools, clients/collaborators, products, monthly goals, daily tasks, specialization details, and the eventual professional model.

## What this is not

This is not a daily planner, habit tracker, hobby catalogue, or rigid five-year calendar.

The architecture comes first. Execution systems such as Notion and Reclaim can later hold and schedule the work without becoming the architecture itself.
