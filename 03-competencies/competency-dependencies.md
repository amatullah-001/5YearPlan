# Competency Dependencies

## Health expertise

**Depends on:** Nursing education, anatomy/physiology/pathology/medical-surgical/public-health foundations, clinical reasoning, continued learning.

**Evidence:** academic performance, applied work, accurate health outputs, supervised/professional evidence where applicable.

## Evidence and research literacy

**Depends on:** research methods, information retrieval, critical appraisal, statistics/epidemiology, citation, repeated appraisal of real papers.

**Evidence:** literature reviews, evidence summaries, research assignments, independently justified health work.

## Nutrition

**Depends on:** foundational nutrition knowledge, physiology/metabolism, evidence appraisal, investigation of professional scope, and potentially formal nutrition/dietetics education if the eventual practice scope requires it.

**Evidence:** accurate nutrition work, evidence syntheses, relevant academic/professional qualification where required.

## Natural/traditional health knowledge

**Depends on:** structured exposure to relevant traditions, careful source handling, evidence appraisal, safety/interaction knowledge, and scope/qualification investigation.

**Evidence:** disciplined comparative analysis, appropriately sourced work, and supervised/professional evidence if the domain later becomes part of practice.

## Tibb Nabawi

**Depends on:** sound Islamic learning, source distinction, understanding of later scholarly/medical interpretation, and disciplined separation between religious authenticity and clinical evidence.

**Evidence:** accurate personal study notes, source distinctions, and responsible communication where appropriate.

Tibb Nabawi does not require professional application to be valuable.

## Whole-person reasoning and scope judgment

**Depends on:** clinical/health knowledge, contextual assessment, evidence appraisal, safety awareness, professional boundaries, and referral judgment.

**Evidence:** case reasoning, appropriately scoped work, documented referral/safety decisions where applicable, and feedback from qualified professionals or supervisors when available.

## Translation and communication

**Depends on:** understanding audience needs, writing, explanation, visual communication, instructional/intervention design, iterative testing.

**Evidence:** deployed work, user feedback, revisions, comprehension or other outcomes where measurable.

## Digital delivery

**Depends on:** product thinking, basic web/app literacy, interaction design, content structure, collaboration with developers when deeper engineering is required.

**Evidence:** functioning prototypes/products, documented design decisions, shipped health experiences.

## Remote collaboration

**Depends on:** written communication, project coordination, professional reliability, async work habits, clear handoffs.

**Evidence:** completed collaborations, references, shipped work.

## Integration

**health knowledge + evidence + whole-person reasoning + relevant nutrition/natural/traditional knowledge + translation + appropriate delivery → useful, safe, appropriately scoped health work.**

No single supporting domain is assumed to become the deepest professional domain in advance.
